## Common Language When Buying From Importers

EXW = FROM WORKS - you will need to go to the factory and pick up the goods by yourself

FCA = FREE CARRIER - it is the seller’s obligation to hand the goods over to the first carrier at the named place once they have been cleared for export

FOB = FREE ONBOARD VESSEL - suppliers will bring the goods to the port, clear for export and collect onto the ship

FAS = FREE ALONGSIDE SHIP - suppliers will have goods at the dock nearby the factory

CIF = COST INSURANCE FREIGHT - in this case you will need to collect all the goods from the destination port

DDP = DELIVER DUTY PAID - you will only need to wait at your doorstep

More Info on Incoterms Here -> https://www.universalcargo.com/incoterms-definitions-part-1-exw-fca-fas-fob/
